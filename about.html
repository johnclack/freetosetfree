Who We Are

Helping people move toward freedom

Free to Set Free is a Christian nonprofit devoted to encouraging people facing life-controlling struggles and helping them find practical support, renewed hope, and purpose.

We believe every person deserves dignity, compassion, and the opportunity to take a meaningful next step.

Our approach

We combine faith-centered encouragement with practical resources and referrals. We do not replace licensed medical, legal, financial, or mental-health professionals.

Contact us at freedom@freetosetfree.org.
